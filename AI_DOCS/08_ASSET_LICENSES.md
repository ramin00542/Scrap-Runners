# Asset License Log

Every non-code IMAGE/AUDIO/FONT asset must have a complete entry here at
the moment it is committed — never before, never with blank fields.
Purely `.tscn`-defined visuals (`Polygon2D`, `ColorRect`) do NOT require
an entry, since they are not separate binary files.
